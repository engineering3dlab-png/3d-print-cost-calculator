Technician Mode Instructions

Use the calculator on the left and the email fields on the right to prepare a message for Gmail.

  1. Choose an email template. Pricing quotes require a cost calculation and estimated completion time. Sponsored-event and class-print messages do not.
  2. Enter the recipient and print details. The subject and email preview update automatically.
  3. Calculate Cost after entering or changing print quantities and machine time. This updates the quote in the email.
  4. Copy Recipient Address and Copy Subject to fill in the Gmail message fields.
  5. Copy Formatted Email and paste it into the Gmail message body. Review the pasted formatting, especially the pickup image.
  6. Regenerate Preview restores the email from the current fields and removes any manual edits made in the preview.

Before sending: Check the recipient, message, links, price (if applicable), and any images in Gmail. Nothing is sent automatically.
